Welcome to BookerOS Web

An operating system where every app is a web app. Here's how to get around.

⭕
LauncherClick the circle at the bottom-left, or tap the Windows key, to search apps and the web.
📌
ShelfPinned apps sit at the bottom. Right-click an icon to unpin it; pin more from the launcher.
🛍️
App StoreTurn any website into an app with its own window, icon and name. Firefox is your full browser.
🕘
Quick SettingsClick the clock at the bottom-right for dark theme, accent colour, network and power.
📁
Your filesNotes, Paint and Files save into your Documents folder.
⌨️
ShortcutsAlt+Tab switch · Ctrl+W close · Win+↑ maximise · F11 full screen (Esc to leave).
